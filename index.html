Connect Airtable

Your token is encrypted on this device with your passphrase. It is never saved in the code or sent anywhere except Airtable.

  1. Create a base with a table named Items (fields listed in the README).
  2. Create a personal access token with only data.records:read and data.records:write, and give it access to that one base.
  3. Copy the base ID (starts with app) from the base's URL.

Set aside cash

Cash you’re keeping for something else, e.g. business capital or a house deposit. It’s taken off your total cash before working out your emergency fund.

What counts as your emergency fund?

By default, every holding in the Cash bucket. Pick holdings yourself if some cash isn’t really available, or if you’d count other things. Your choice is saved in this browser.

Exchange rates

Holdings in other currencies are converted into your display currency with these rates. The daily sync refreshes them (currencies from the European Central Bank, bitcoin and gold from Alpha Vantage); a rate you type in yourself is kept until you reset it.
